Need help?

Here are some quick answers to keep your storytelling flow uninterrupted.

How do I start a new story?

Select the pencil icon in the sidebar or click “New Post” on your profile page to open the editor.

Can I collaborate with others?

Collaboration tools are coming soon. For now, share drafts by exporting and inviting feedback.

Where are my drafts saved?

Drafts are saved to your workspace automatically. Visit the Drafts tab on your profile to view them.
